## REQUEST (for Codex)

You are the concept artist for **Mini Midgard**, an original 2D fantasy idle RPG for mobile (portrait). Use your **image generation tool** to create the 5 concept images below. This round is for **style approval only** — the client will pick a direction before any production sprites are made, so prioritise *art direction quality and consistency* over quantity.

The attached image `docs/art/ref/current-lineup.png` shows the **current in-game placeholder art** (simple code-drawn chibis, 2.5 heads tall). Keep each class's identity (role, colour coding, weapon, silhouette cues) but raise the quality far above it. Do not copy its drawing style.

### Art direction (applies to every image)

**Overall feel.** Japanese manga/anime character design with the warm, charming, slightly nostalgic mood of classic 2000s 2D fantasy MMORPG illustration — sunny storybook fantasy, adventurers you want to collect and dress up. It should look like an official *character design sheet (settei)* from a professional Japanese game studio: art-book quality, clean and confident.

**Proportions.** Super-deformed but elegant: **3 heads tall** (option A) — head ≈ 1/3 of total height, compact torso, short but clearly readable legs, small hands that can still hold weapons. (Image 01 also shows a 2.5-heads option B for comparison.) Every character in a sheet must share exactly the same head size, eye line and ground line.

**Line art.** Crisp, clean, hand-inked anime lines with **tapered line weight**: a slightly heavier outer silhouette line, thin inner detail lines. **Coloured line art** (deep warm brown / dark plum / dark navy depending on the area), never flat pure black everywhere.

**Colouring.** Anime **cel shading**: base + 1–2 hard-edged shadow tones + small crisp highlights; very subtle soft gradient only on hair and large cloth areas; light from upper-left. Hair has a glossy "angel ring" highlight band. Harmonious, saturated-but-not-neon palettes; warm skin with soft cheek blush.

**Faces.** Large expressive anime eyes (tall oval irises with 2 highlights, darker upper lash line, coloured iris gradient), tiny nose hint, small expressive mouth. Two face types: **Type A** (softer, rounder, feminine) and **Type B** (sharper brows, slightly narrower eyes, masculine). Cheerful, confident expressions — personality in every face.

**Hair.** Distinct anime hair clumps with pointed strand tips, layered front bangs, an ahoge on some characters; hair silhouettes must differ clearly between characters.

**Costumes.** Original medieval-European fantasy adventurer outfits. Each class readable by **silhouette + colour block** at small size. Tasteful detail: belts, pouches, buckles, embroidered trims, layered fabric, leather straps, simple bevelled metal plates, cuffed boots. Detailed but *not* cluttered — big shapes first, small details second.

**Pose & view.** 3/4 front view, body turned toward the viewer's right (the game sprites face right). Relaxed confident idle stance holding the class weapon (weapon in the character's right hand = the hand toward the viewer's right/front; shield on the other arm).

**Production constraints (design with these in mind).** Final in-game sprites are only **~64–90 px tall** on a phone (rendered at 2–3× for retina) and are built as **paper-doll layers**: back hair / body+outfit / arms / head / front hair / headgear (sits on top of the hair) / weapon / shield / cape. So: strong silhouettes, big readable head, clear colour blocking, no essential detail smaller than a few pixels at game size, hair that does not hide hands or weapons, capes and long cloth that could live on a separate back layer.

**Background & layout.** Plain warm off-white / light parchment background, soft grey oval ground shadow under each character, generous spacing, all characters standing on one shared ground line at identical scale. **No text, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no UI frames** (the client maps positions using this brief).

**Avoid (these read as "cheap"):** flat vector clip-art; uniform thick black outlines; generic sticker/emoji chibis with dot eyes; 3D-render or plastic/glossy toy shading; over-airbrushed gradients; muddy or desaturated palettes; neon oversaturation; western-cartoon faces; realistic anatomy; inconsistent proportions or head sizes between characters; smudgy AI artefacts, melted details, extra fingers, broken weapons; random gibberish lettering. Do **not** reproduce any existing game's characters, mascots, sprites or logos (especially not Ragnarok Online's) — everything must be original.

### Process
- Generate each image separately with the image generation tool, using a detailed prompt built from the art direction above.
- Review every result against the **Avoid** list and the consistency rules (same head size / ground line / style across a sheet). If an image fails, regenerate it (up to 2 retries per image) and keep the best one.
- Copy the final images into `docs/art/concepts/round1/` (create the folder). Write the exact final prompt you used for each image into `docs/art/concepts/round1/prompts.md`, with one or two lines of self-critique per image (what works, what should change next round).
- Do not modify any other files in the repository.
- Finish with a short summary in Korean listing the files you produced.
