## 사용한 프롬프트

Use case: precise-object-edit. Redesign the first reference game character parts sheet into a production transparent sprite asset atlas. The second reference is the LOCKED visual style: rounded compact chibi, thick dark brown outline, flat warm pastel colors, no shine or anime detail. Preserve the white-haired Novice girl's design and the blue silver Swordsman outfit from the first sheet. Deliver a square image, 4 columns by 4 rows of isolated non-touching components with generous empty spacing, TRANSPARENT background, no labels no text no guides no assembled character. Contents exact row order: row1 bald face head (3/4 facing right, blue eyes, warm skin, NO neck), cream white front hair with cowlick and open face hole, matching rear hair silhouette, ochre novice torso with brown belt and split tunic hem (no arms, no legs). Row2: near novice arm (short bent warm-skin arm with ochre short sleeve, brown wrist cuff, small fist, rounded shoulder overlap), far novice arm (same shorter shaded arm), near leg (very SHORT bare thigh with gray sock band and chunky brown boot, NO shorts or skirt at top), far leg (same short shaded booted leg). Row3: blue silver swordsman torso (same compact tunic dimensions, silver chestplate, blue split hem, brown belt red tabard), short bent swordsman arm (blue sleeve and silver shoulder, cuff fist), isolated dagger horizontal blade points right (brown grip yellow guard silver blade), isolated sword horizontal blade points right (brown grip yellow guard silver blade). Row4: leaf sprout accessory, yellow star hairpin accessory, alternative novice arm extended horizontally right with small closed fist (no weapon), alternative swordsman arm extended horizontally right with small closed fist (no weapon). All complete objects uncut. The head+hair assembly should be about half total character height; body torso height about 2/3 face height, visible leg beneath hem about 1/2 face height; short chunky limbs, NEVER stick limbs. Opaque clean color inside each object and true alpha outside. Keep painted simplicity, dark warm brown outlines, no glow or drop shadows, no magenta. This is a reference-matching production atlas, not a concept redesign.
